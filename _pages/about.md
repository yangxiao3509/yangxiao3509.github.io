I am a postdoctoral scholar in the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca) at [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca), working with [Prof. Raouf Boutaba](https://rboutaba.cs.uwaterloo.ca). Before joining Waterloo, I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering in the [School of Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.bupt.edu.cn/en/) at [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://english.bupt.edu.cn), where I was advised by Prof. Jun Liu. I also received my B.Eng. in Electronic and Information Engineering in the [School of Information and Communication Engineering](https://sice.bupt.edu.cn/#) at BUPT.
