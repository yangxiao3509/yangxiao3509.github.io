I am a postdoctoral scholar in the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca) at the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca), working with [Prof. Raouf Boutaba](https://rboutaba.cs.uwaterloo.ca). Before joining Waterloo, I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from the [School of Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.bupt.edu.cn/en/) at [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://english.bupt.edu.cn), advised by Prof. Jun Liu. I also received my B.Eng. in Electronic and Information Engineering from the [School of Information and Communication Engineering](https://sice.bupt.edu.cn/#) at BUPT, graduating with highest honors in both programs.

My research centers on **AI-driven autonomous networks**, with a focus on how learning and coordination enable networks to adapt to changing conditions while meeting diverse service requirements. I combine **multi-agent reinforcement learning, game theory, and network systems experimentation** to develop adaptive routing, traffic engineering, and resource management methods for wired and wireless networks. My current interests include agentic radio access network (RAN), intelligent Open RAN slicing, and adaptive multi-agent coordination for multi-objective resource management. Across these areas, I aim to connect learning-based decision-making with practical network operation through reproducible packet-level simulation and programmable testbeds.

My latest [curriculum vitae]({{ '/files/cv.pdf' | relative_url }}) provides further details on my research, publication, and academic activities.
